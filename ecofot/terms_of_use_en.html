1. Purpose and operation of EcoFot AI

EcoFot AI is an application developed by Aplicaciones HerrBri to help identify the recommended disposal destination for objects and waste through photo analysis.

The user takes or selects a photo. To analyze it, the application sends it to a server operated by the developer, which uses the Google Gemini API. The information obtained is interpreted in accordance with the instructions and classification rules prepared for the country and, where applicable, region selected by the user. The application then provides a recommendation for a waste container or collection location, together with an explanation.

An Internet connection is required for image analysis. An EcoFot AI user account is not required.

2. Guidance only and AI limitations

Results are provided as guidance. Visual identification of waste may be incorrect or incomplete. A photo may not reveal the actual material, contents, condition or presence of special substances.

Waste collection and recycling rules may differ between countries, regions, municipalities and waste operators, and may change over time. Users must check that the correct country and region have been selected.

When in doubt, particularly for hazardous, chemical, medical, electrical, electronic or specially regulated waste, the official instructions of the competent authorities and waste management services take precedence. EcoFot AI does not replace those instructions.
